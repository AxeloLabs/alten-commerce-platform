== Payment

Payment and refund are never started by a person.
They are steps of an order: each is included in the use cases that need it and cannot be started on its own.
Their primary actor is the customer on whose behalf they run.

[[UC-PAY-001]]
=== UC-PAY-001 — Take a payment

{critical}

[cols="3,11"]
|===
| Overview | The platform charges the total of an order on the customer's means of payment, through the payment provider. In this release the provider is simulated and its answers are scripted by the token.
| Primary actor | Customer, indirectly: the use case is included in {UC-ORD-001}
| Supporting actors | Payment provider
| Goal | Capture the order total exactly once.
| Priority and status | {must} · {st-draft}
| Trigger | The stock of the order is reserved.
| Preconditions | The order is `PENDING` with its stock reserved. The request carries the order identifier, the amount and the payment token.
| Postconditions on success | One payment is `CAPTURED` for the order, for the order total.
| Postconditions in every case | An order is charged at most once, however many times the request is received. The payment always ends in a known state — captured, refused or canceled — before its order is rejected. No card data is stored.
| Authorization | {denied} Every person. A payment is requested only by the placement of an order.
| Business rules | {FR-PAY-001} {FR-PAY-002} {FR-PAY-003} {FR-PAY-004} {FR-PAY-005} {FR-PAY-007}
| Quality attributes | {NFR-REL-002} {NFR-SEC-003} {NFR-SEC-007} {NFR-OBS-001}
| Stories | {US-PAY-001}
| Acceptance | {BDD-PAY-001} {BDD-PAY-002} {BDD-PAY-003} {BDD-PAY-004} {BDD-PAY-007} {BDD-PAY-008} {BDD-PAY-009} {BDD-PAY-010}
|===

.Main success scenario
. The order placement requests a payment for an order, an amount and a token.
. The system records the payment as `PENDING`.
. The system asks the provider to charge the amount.
. The provider accepts.
. The system records the payment as `CAPTURED` and answers the order placement.

.Alternative flows
1a. A payment already exists for the order:: The system answers with the recorded outcome. The provider is not called again.
3a. The provider is slow or fails transiently:: The system retries a limited number of times, with the same reference so that the provider itself cannot charge twice.

.Exception flows
4a. The provider refuses:: The payment is recorded as `REFUSED` with the provider's reason. The order placement is told.
3b. The provider does not answer after the retries:: The payment is recorded as `UNKNOWN`. The order placement is told that nothing is established yet.
3c. The provider is failing repeatedly:: Further calls are suspended for a while instead of piling up; requests received meanwhile are answered `UNKNOWN`.

.Canceling a payment whose outcome is unknown
. At the order deadline, the order placement asks to cancel the payment.
. The system asks the provider to cancel it, with the same reference.
. The provider confirms. The payment is recorded as `CANCELED`: it can no longer be captured.

.Alternative and exception flows of the cancellation
3d. The provider answers "already captured":: The payment is recorded as `CAPTURED` and the order is carried through.
3e. The provider does not answer:: The payment stays `UNKNOWN` and the cancellation is retried. If nothing is established when the review delay expires, the order goes under review.

[[UC-PAY-002]]
=== UC-PAY-002 — Refund a payment

{critical}

[cols="3,11"]
|===
| Overview | The platform gives back a captured payment in full: because the stock of a paid order was lost, because an administrator closed an order under review, or because the customer canceled.
| Primary actor | Customer, indirectly: the use case is included in {UC-ORD-001}, {UC-ORD-004} and {UC-ORD-007}
| Supporting actors | Payment provider
| Goal | Return the full amount exactly once.
| Priority and status | {must} · {st-draft}
| Trigger | An order with a captured payment must be rejected or canceled.
| Preconditions | The payment is `CAPTURED`.
| Postconditions on success | The payment is `REFUNDED` for the full captured amount, and the customer is told by e-mail.
| Postconditions in every case | A payment is refunded at most once. A refund that could not be made is never recorded as made.
| Authorization | {denied} Every person directly. An administrator triggers it through {UC-ORD-007}, a customer through {UC-ORD-004}.
| Business rules | {FR-PAY-006} {FR-PAY-005} {FR-ORD-008} {FR-ORD-014}
| Quality attributes | {NFR-REL-002} {NFR-OBS-004}
| Stories | {US-PAY-002} {US-PAY-003}
| Acceptance | {BDD-PAY-005} {BDD-PAY-006} {BDD-ORD-029}
|===

.Main success scenario
. A refund of the payment of an order is requested.
. The system asks the provider to refund the captured amount.
. The provider accepts.
. The system records the payment as `REFUNDED` and answers.

.Alternative flows
1a. The payment is already refunded:: The system answers "refunded". The provider is not called again.
1b. The payment was refused, canceled or never captured:: There is nothing to refund; the system answers so.

.Exception flows
3a. The provider refuses or does not answer:: The system retries. After the retries the refund is reported as failed; the order goes, or stays, `UNDER_REVIEW`.

== Notification

[[UC-NOT-001]]
=== UC-NOT-001 — Notify the customer by e-mail

{critical}

[cols="3,11"]
|===
| Overview | When something the customer should know happens to an order, the platform sends an e-mail. Notification listens to what happened; it is never in the way of the order.
| Primary actor | Customer, as recipient. The use case is triggered by what happens to an order, never by a person.
| Supporting actors | E-mail provider
| Goal | Inform the customer once, promptly and truthfully.
| Priority and status | {must} · {st-draft}
| Trigger | An order is confirmed, rejected or put under review, or a payment is refunded; and, when those journeys are accepted, an order is canceled, shipped or delivered.
| Preconditions | The event carries the order number, the customer's e-mail address and what the e-mail must say.
| Postconditions on success | One e-mail was handed to the e-mail provider for the event.
| Postconditions in every case | At most one e-mail per event. A failure to notify never changes or delays the order. No payment data appears in an e-mail. An e-mail never says "nothing was charged" when something was.
| Authorization | {denied} Every person. An e-mail is triggered only by what happens to an order.
| Business rules | {FR-NOT-001} {FR-NOT-002} {FR-NOT-003} {FR-NOT-004}
| Quality attributes | {NFR-REL-002} {NFR-SEC-007} {NFR-AVAIL-003}
| Stories | {US-NOT-001} {US-NOT-002} {US-NOT-003}
| Acceptance | {BDD-NOT-001} {BDD-NOT-002} {BDD-NOT-003} {BDD-NOT-004} {BDD-NOT-005}
|===

.Main success scenario
. Something happens to an order.
. The system builds the e-mail for it: order number, lines, total, new status; for a rejection, the reason in plain language and whether anything was charged and refunded.
. The system hands the e-mail to the provider.
. The system records the e-mail as sent.

.Alternative flows
1a. The same event is received again:: Nothing is sent.

.Exception flows
3a. The provider fails:: The system retries later, with increasing delays. After the last retry the e-mail is recorded as not sent and an alert is raised. The order is not affected.
