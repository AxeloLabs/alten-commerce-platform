== Order

Orders are where the platform makes its promise; these use cases carry most of the business rules.

[[UC-ORD-001]]
=== UC-ORD-001 — Place an order

{critical}

[cols="3,11"]
|===
| Overview | The customer turns the cart into an order. The system secures the stock, takes the payment and confirms the order, or rejects it and undoes whatever it had done. It is the use case the whole platform is built around.
| Primary actor | Customer
| Supporting actors | Payment provider, e-mail provider, time
| Goal | Own a confirmed, paid order, or know why not and have paid nothing.
| Priority and status | {must} · {st-draft}
| Trigger | The customer proceeds to checkout.
| Preconditions | The customer is signed in. The cart holds at least one line.
| Success guarantees a|
* The order is `CONFIRMED`, with the lines and prices shown at checkout.
* Exactly one payment was captured, for exactly the order total.
* The stock on hand of each product decreased by the quantity ordered.
* The cart is empty and a confirmation e-mail is on its way.
| Minimal guarantees a|
* The order ends `CONFIRMED`, `REJECTED` with a reason, or `UNDER_REVIEW`; it never stays `PENDING` beyond the deadline.
* No payment stays captured for an order that is not confirmed: it is refunded, or the order is under review with an alert.
* No stock stays set aside for a rejected order.
* Whatever is retried, duplicated or restarted, there is at most one order and one payment per checkout.
| Authorization | {allowed} Customer, from their own cart. {denied} Visitor. Administrator.
| Business rules | {FR-ORD-001} {FR-ORD-002} {FR-ORD-003} {FR-ORD-004} {FR-ORD-005} {FR-ORD-006} {FR-ORD-007} {FR-ORD-008} {FR-ORD-009} {FR-ORD-010} {FR-ORD-011} {FR-ORD-014} {FR-ORD-015} {FR-INV-003} {FR-INV-004} {FR-INV-005} {FR-CART-007}
| Quality attributes | {NFR-PERF-002} {NFR-PERF-003} {NFR-REL-001} {NFR-REL-002} {NFR-REL-003} {NFR-OBS-001}
| Stories | {US-ORD-001} {US-ORD-002} {US-ORD-003} {US-ORD-004} {US-ORD-005}
| Acceptance | {BDD-ORD-001} {BDD-ORD-002} {BDD-ORD-003} {BDD-ORD-004} {BDD-ORD-005} {BDD-ORD-006} {BDD-ORD-007} {BDD-ORD-008} {BDD-ORD-009} {BDD-ORD-010} {BDD-ORD-011} {BDD-ORD-012} {BDD-ORD-023} {BDD-INV-005}
|===

.Main success scenario
. The customer opens the checkout from the cart.
. The system shows the lines with current prices and availability, and the total.
. The customer enters a shipping address and a payment token, and submits.
. The system checks that nothing changed since step 2: every product is still published, at the same price, with enough displayed availability.
. The system creates the order `PENDING`, with an order number and the prices copied from the catalog, and acknowledges it to the customer at once.
. The system reserves the stock of every line.
. The system charges the order total ({UC-PAY-001}).
. The system confirms the reservation; the stock on hand decreases.
. The system sets the order to `CONFIRMED` and empties the cart.
. The system sends the confirmation e-mail ({UC-NOT-001}).
. The storefront, which has been asking for the order status since step 5, shows the order as confirmed.

.Alternative flows
3a. The customer submits the same checkout again (double click, refresh, retry after a network error):: The system recognizes the checkout and returns the order created the first time, in its current state. Nothing new is created.
7a. The payment answer is slow but arrives before the deadline:: The flow continues at step 8.
8a. The inventory is temporarily unavailable:: The system retries the confirmation until it succeeds. The payment is captured, so the order goes forward; the customer keeps seeing "pending" meanwhile, and an alert is raised if it lasts.

.Exception flows
1a. The cart is empty:: Checkout is refused.
3b. The shipping address or the payment token is missing or malformed:: Nothing is created. Every faulty field is reported.
4a. A price changed, a product was retired or the displayed availability is insufficient:: No order is created. The differences are returned and the customer is sent back to the cart.
6a. One line cannot be reserved in full:: Nothing is reserved. The order becomes `REJECTED` with the reason `OUT_OF_STOCK`. No payment is requested. The cart is kept.
6b. The inventory does not answer before the deadline:: The order becomes `REJECTED` with the reason `TIMED_OUT`. A release is sent in case the reservation was made after all. The cart is kept.
7b. The payment is refused:: The reservation is released. The order becomes `REJECTED` with the reason `PAYMENT_REFUSED`. The cart is kept.
7c. No payment answer before the deadline:: The system asks for the payment status. Not captured: the reservation is released and the order becomes `REJECTED` with the reason `TIMED_OUT`. Captured: the flow continues at step 8.
7d. A "captured" answer arrives after the order was rejected:: The payment is refunded ({UC-PAY-002}). The order stays `REJECTED` and shows the refund.
8b. The reservation no longer exists and the stock cannot be reserved again:: The payment is refunded. The order becomes `REJECTED` with the reason `OUT_OF_STOCK`.
*a. At any step, a release or a refund keeps failing:: The order becomes `UNDER_REVIEW` and an administrator is alerted ({UC-ORD-007}).
*b. At any step, the order service stops and restarts:: The order resumes from the last step it recorded.

In every rejection the customer is told the reason on screen and by e-mail, and that nothing was charged or that the charge is being refunded.

|===
| Overview | The customer lists their orders.
| Primary actor | Customer
| Supporting actors | –
| Goal | Find any past or current order.
| Priority and status | {must} · {st-draft}
| Trigger | The customer opens "My orders".
| Preconditions | The customer is signed in.
| Success guarantees | The list shows the customer's orders, newest first, with number, date, total and status.
| Minimal guarantees | No order of another customer is ever listed.
| Authorization | {allowed} Customer, own orders. {denied} Visitor.

| Quality attributes | {NFR-PERF-001}

| Acceptance | {BDD-ORD-013}
|===

.Main success scenario
. The customer opens the order history.
. The system returns the first page of the customer's orders, newest first.
. The customer asks for another page or opens an order ({UC-ORD-003}).

.Alternative flows
2a. The customer has no order:: The system returns an empty list.

[[UC-ORD-003]]
=== UC-ORD-003 — Track an order

[cols="3,11"]
|===
| Overview | The customer opens an order to see its content, its status and how it got there. The same use case serves the screen that waits for the outcome right after checkout.
| Primary actor | Customer (also administrator, for any order)
| Supporting actors | –
| Goal | Know where the order stands.
| Priority and status | {must} · {st-draft}
| Trigger | The customer opens an order, or the storefront refreshes a pending order.
| Preconditions | The actor is signed in.
| Success guarantees | The order shows its lines, the prices paid, the shipping address, the current status and the date of each status change. A rejected order shows its reason; a refunded payment shows the refund.
| Minimal guarantees | The status shown is one of the order lifecycle; once final it never changes.
| Authorization | {allowed} Customer, own orders. Administrator, any order. {denied} Visitor. Another customer's order: "not found".
| Business rules | {FR-IAM-004} {FR-ORD-010} {FR-ORD-011}
| Quality attributes | {NFR-PERF-001} {NFR-USAB-002}
| Stories | {US-ORD-007} {US-ORD-003}
| Acceptance | {BDD-ORD-014} {BDD-ORD-022}
|===

.Main success scenario
. The actor opens an order by its number.
. The system returns the order with its status and its history.

.Alternative flows
2a. The order is pending:: The storefront asks again at short intervals until the status is final, then stops.

.Exception flows
2b. The order does not exist or belongs to another customer:: The system answers "not found".

[[UC-ORD-004]]
=== UC-ORD-004 — Cancel an order

{st-proposed}

[cols="3,11"]
|===
| Overview | The customer cancels a confirmed order that has not shipped. The stock is returned and the payment refunded in full.
| Primary actor | Customer
| Supporting actors | Payment provider, e-mail provider
| Goal | Undo a purchase at no cost.
| Priority and status | {should} · {st-proposed}
| Trigger | The customer chooses "cancel" on an order.
| Preconditions | The customer is signed in. The order is `CONFIRMED`.
| Success guarantees | The order is `CANCELLED`. The quantities ordered are available again. The full amount is refunded and the customer is told.
| Minimal guarantees | An order is cancelled at most once and refunded at most once. From the moment the cancellation is accepted the order can no longer ship. The order becomes `CANCELLED` only once the refund is made; if the refund keeps failing, the order goes `UNDER_REVIEW`.
| Authorization | {allowed} Customer, own order. {denied} Visitor. Administrator. Another customer's order: "not found".
| Business rules | {FR-ORD-012} {FR-ORD-010} {FR-ORD-014} {FR-PAY-006} {FR-IAM-004}
| Quality attributes | {NFR-REL-001} {NFR-REL-002}
| Stories | {US-ORD-008} {US-PAY-003}
| Acceptance | {BDD-ORD-015} {BDD-ORD-016}
|===

.Main success scenario
. The customer asks to cancel an order.
. The system checks that the order is `CONFIRMED` and has not shipped.
. The system records the cancellation; from now on the order cannot ship.
. The system returns the quantities to the stock.
. The system refunds the payment ({UC-PAY-002}).
. The system sets the order to `CANCELLED` and tells the customer, on screen and by e-mail.

.Alternative flows
1a. The customer asks twice:: The second request returns the order, cancelled or being cancelled; nothing is done twice.

.Exception flows
2a. The order has shipped, or is not confirmed:: The cancellation is refused and the reason is given.
5a. The refund keeps failing:: The order becomes `UNDER_REVIEW` ({UC-ORD-007}).

[[UC-ORD-006]]
=== UC-ORD-006 — Update the fulfillment of an order

{st-proposed}

[cols="3,11"]
|===
| Overview | The administrator records that an order has shipped, then that it was delivered.
| Primary actor | Administrator
| Supporting actors | E-mail provider
| Goal | Take a confirmed order to delivery.
| Priority and status | {should} · {st-proposed}
| Trigger | The parcel leaves, or arrives.
| Preconditions | The administrator is signed in. The order is `CONFIRMED` (to ship) or `SHIPPED` (to deliver).
| Success guarantees | The order is `SHIPPED`, then `DELIVERED`. Each change is recorded with its author and date, and the customer is told.
| Minimal guarantees | No status is skipped or revisited. A shipped order can no longer be cancelled.
| Authorization | {allowed} Administrator. {denied} Visitor, customer.
| Business rules | {FR-ORD-013} {FR-ORD-010}
| Quality attributes | {NFR-SEC-006}
| Stories | {US-ORD-010} {US-ORD-011}
| Acceptance | {BDD-ORD-018} {BDD-ORD-019}
|===

.Main success scenario
. The administrator marks a confirmed order as shipped.
. The system sets the order to `SHIPPED` and tells the customer.
. The administrator marks the order as delivered.
. The system sets the order to `DELIVERED` and tells the customer.

.Exception flows
1a. The order is not `CONFIRMED`:: Shipping is refused.
3a. The order is not `SHIPPED`:: Delivery is refused.
1b. The customer cancelled the order a moment before:: Shipping is refused; the order is `CANCELLED`.

[[UC-ORD-007]]
=== UC-ORD-007 — Resolve an order under review

{critical}

[cols="3,11"]
|===
| Overview | An order is under review when the system could not undo, or could not complete, what it had started: a refund or a stock release kept failing. A person decides.
| Primary actor | Administrator
| Supporting actors | Payment provider
| Goal | Bring the order to a state the business and the customer can live with.
| Priority and status | {must} · {st-draft}
| Trigger | An alert says an order is under review.
| Preconditions | The administrator is signed in. The order is `UNDER_REVIEW`.
| Success guarantees | The order left `UNDER_REVIEW` for `CONFIRMED` or `REJECTED`. Stock and payment match that status. The decision, its author and a comment are recorded. The customer is told.
| Minimal guarantees | A manual decision goes through the same rules as the automatic flow: a payment is never refunded twice, stock is never released twice.
| Authorization | {allowed} Administrator. {denied} Visitor, customer.
| Business rules | {FR-ORD-014} {FR-ORD-010} {FR-PAY-006} {FR-IAM-005}
| Quality attributes | {NFR-SEC-006} {NFR-OBS-004} {NFR-REL-003}
| Stories | {US-ORD-012}
| Acceptance | {BDD-ORD-012} {BDD-ORD-020} {BDD-ORD-021}
|===

.Main success scenario
. The administrator opens the orders under review.
. The system lists them with, for each, the steps done, the step that failed and why.
. The administrator asks to retry the failed step.
. The system retries it; it succeeds, and the order continues to its outcome.

.Alternative flows
3a. The administrator decides to reject the order:: The system refunds any captured payment, releases any reserved stock, and sets the order to `REJECTED`.
3b. The administrator decides to confirm the order, after settling the stock by hand:: The system sets the order to `CONFIRMED`.

.Exception flows
4a. The retry fails again:: The order stays `UNDER_REVIEW`; the new failure is added to its history.
3c. No comment is given:: The decision is refused.
