== Inventory

.Strategy question
****
What strategy describes how stock is entered, corrected and consulted?
****

The inventory is the only place that knows what can really be sold.

[[UC-INV-001]]
=== UC-INV-001 — Adjust stock

{critical}

[cols="3,11"]
|===
| Overview | The administrator records that goods were received, counted, lost or damaged. It is the only way stock enters the store in this release.
| Primary actor | Administrator
| Supporting actors | –
| Goal | Make the stock known to the store equal to the stock really held.
| Priority and status | {must} · {st-draft}
| Trigger | A delivery, a stock count, a loss.
| Preconditions | The administrator is signed in. The product exists.
| Postconditions on success | The stock on hand is changed by the quantity given. The movement is recorded with its reason, author and date. The storefront availability follows within seconds.
| Postconditions in every case | The stock on hand never goes below the quantity reserved for orders in progress, and never below zero.
| Authorization | {allowed} Administrator. {denied} Visitor and customer.
| Business rules | {FR-INV-001} {FR-INV-002} {FR-INV-006} {FR-INV-007} {FR-INV-008} {FR-INV-009}
| Quality attributes | {NFR-SEC-006} {NFR-REL-001}
| Stories | {US-INV-001} {US-INV-003}
| Acceptance | {BDD-INV-001} {BDD-INV-002} {BDD-INV-003} {BDD-INV-006}
|===

.Main success scenario
. The administrator selects a product and enters a quantity to add or remove, with a reason.
. The system checks that the resulting stock on hand is not below the reserved quantity.
. The system records the movement and the new stock on hand.
. The system publishes the new available quantity; the catalog refreshes the displayed availability.

.Alternative flows
1a. The administrator enters the counted quantity instead of a difference:: The system computes the difference and continues at step 2.

.Exception flows
2a. The adjustment would bring the stock on hand below the reserved quantity:: The adjustment is refused; the reserved quantity is shown.
1b. The quantity is zero or the reason is missing:: The adjustment is refused.
1c. The catalog does not know the product:: The adjustment is refused: stock is never created for a SKU that is not in the catalog. The check is made the first time stock is entered for a product.
1d. Stock is entered for a product for the first time and the catalog cannot be reached:: The adjustment is refused and can be made again later. Adjustments of products already in stock are not affected.

[[UC-INV-002]]
=== UC-INV-002 — Review stock levels

[cols="3,11"]
|===
| Overview | The administrator sees, for every product, what is held, what is set aside for orders in progress and what can still be sold.
| Primary actor | Administrator
| Supporting actors | –
| Goal | Know what to replenish.
| Priority and status | {should} · {st-draft}
| Trigger | Routine review, or a customer question.
| Preconditions | The administrator is signed in.
| Postconditions on success | The list shows, per SKU, the stock on hand, the reserved quantity and the available quantity.
| Postconditions in every case | The figures are those of the inventory itself, not the displayed availability of the catalog.
| Authorization | {allowed} Administrator. {denied} Visitor and customer.
| Business rules | {FR-INV-002} {FR-CAT-008}
| Quality attributes | {NFR-PERF-001}
| Stories | {US-INV-002}
| Acceptance | {BDD-INV-004}
|===

.Main success scenario
. The administrator opens the stock review.
. The system returns the stock levels page by page.
. The administrator limits the list to products that are low or out of stock.
. The system returns the matching products.
