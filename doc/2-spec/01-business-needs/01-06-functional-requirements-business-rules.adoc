== How rules are written

.Strategy question
****
What strategy turns business intentions into statements that a test can prove right or wrong?
****

A functional requirement is *one sentence, one behavior, one verdict*.
It uses "shall", names no screen and no technology, and can be checked by a test that either passes or fails.
Use cases say *when* a rule applies; this document says *what* the rule is, once.

Every rule below is the root of tests in four families:

Nominal:: The rule applied to an ordinary case.
Error:: The rule refusing what it must refuse.
Edge case:: The rule at its limits: zero, the maximum, the exact threshold.
Invariant:: What stays true whatever happens — after retries, duplicates, concurrency or a restart.

Rules live in the domain code of the service that owns them, with no framework around them, so that each one is proven by unit tests alone.

== Reference values

.Strategy question
****
What strategy keeps limits, delays and statuses identical in the rules, the code and the tests?
****

Limits and delays are configuration values with the defaults below.
A rule quotes the name of the value; changing a default changes no rule.

.Limits and delays
[%unbreakable, cols="6,3,8", options="header"]
|===
| Value | Default | Used by
| Price range | 0.01 to 99,999.99 EUR | {FR-CAT-002}
| Page size, default and maximum | 20 and 100 | {FR-CAT-007}
| Low-stock threshold | 5 units | {FR-CAT-008}
| Maximum quantity per cart line | 10 units | {FR-CART-002}
| Maximum number of cart lines | 50 | {FR-CART-002}
| Maximum number of favorites | 100 | {FR-CART-008}
| Reservation lifetime | 15 minutes | {FR-INV-004}
| Order deadline | 2 minutes | {FR-ORD-015}
| Review delay | 5 minutes | {FR-ORD-016}
|===

The three delays are ordered on purpose: an order is decided, or handed to a person, long before its reservation can expire.

.Product status
[%unbreakable, cols="3,12", options="header"]
|===
| Status | Meaning
| `DRAFT` | Being prepared; may still be incomplete. Visible to administrators only.
| `PUBLISHED` | On sale: listed, searchable, can be added to a cart.
| `RETIRED` | Withdrawn for good. Readable only where an order, a cart or a favorite references it, and by administrators.
|===

.Availability of a product
[%unbreakable, cols="3,5,7", options="header"]
|===
| Level | Available quantity known to the catalog | Effect in the storefront
| `IN_STOCK` | More than 5 | Can be added to a cart.
| `LOW_STOCK` | 1 to 5 | Can be added to a cart; the customer is told few units remain.
| `OUT_OF_STOCK` | 0 | Cannot be added to a cart; can be marked as favorite.
|===

The catalog keeps, for each product, a copy of the available quantity announced by the inventory.
That copy can be a few seconds late.
It decides the level shown, and it is what a cart quantity is checked against; it is never a guarantee, which only the reservation made when an order is placed gives.

.Order status
[cols="3,10,2", options="header"]
|===
| Status | Meaning | Final
| `PENDING` | Created; stock reservation and payment are in progress. | No
| `CONFIRMED` | Paid, stock secured. | No
| `REJECTED` | Could not be confirmed. Carries one reason. The order holds no payment: none was captured, or it was refunded. | Yes
| `UNDER_REVIEW` | The system could not decide alone. Automatic processing has stopped; an administrator decides. | No
| `SHIPPED` | Handed to the carrier. | No
| `DELIVERED` | Received by the customer. | Yes
| `CANCELING` | Cancellation accepted; the refund is in progress. The order can no longer ship. | No
| `CANCELED` | Canceled by the customer before shipment; refunded. | Yes
|===

An order is *settled* when it is no longer `PENDING`: the customer has an answer.
A status is *final* when nothing can follow it.

.Rejection reasons
[%unbreakable, cols="4,11", options="header"]
|===
| Reason | Meaning for the customer
| `OUT_OF_STOCK` | A product was no longer available in the quantity ordered. Nothing was charged, or the charge was refunded.
| `PAYMENT_REFUSED` | The payment was declined. Nothing was charged.
| `TIMED_OUT` | The order could not be completed in time. Nothing was charged.
| `ADMIN_DECISION` | An administrator closed the order after a review. Any charge was refunded.
|===

.Payment status
[%unbreakable, cols="3,12", options="header"]
|===
| Status | Meaning
| `PENDING` | Requested; awaiting the provider's answer.
| `CAPTURED` | The provider took the amount.
| `REFUSED` | The provider declined. Final.
| `UNKNOWN` | The provider did not answer; the outcome is not established yet.
| `CANCELED` | Canceled at the provider before any capture; it can no longer be captured. Final.
| `REFUNDED` | The captured amount was returned. Final.
|===

== Inventory

.Strategy question
****
What strategy makes overselling impossible, even when two customers buy the last unit at the same instant?
****

Stock is counted in three quantities.
_On hand_ is what the store holds.
_Reserved_ is what is held for orders being paid.
_Available_ is the difference, and the only quantity customers can buy.

[cols="3,10,2", options="header"]
|===
| ID | Rule | Priority
| [[FR-INV-001]]FR-INV-001 | The stock on hand of a product shall be a whole number, never negative. | {must}
| [[FR-INV-002]]FR-INV-002 | The available quantity shall equal the stock on hand minus the reserved quantity, and shall never be negative. | {must}
| [[FR-INV-003]]FR-INV-003 | A reservation shall cover every line of an order in full, or nothing: a partial reservation shall never exist. | {must}
| [[FR-INV-004]]FR-INV-004 | A reservation that is neither confirmed nor released within the reservation lifetime shall expire, and its quantities shall become available again. | {must}
| [[FR-INV-005]]FR-INV-005 | Confirming a reservation shall decrease the stock on hand and the reserved quantity by the reserved amounts; releasing it shall decrease the reserved quantity only. Confirming a reservation that has expired shall reserve again if the stock allows it, and shall fail otherwise. | {must}
| [[FR-INV-006]]FR-INV-006 | The system shall refuse a stock adjustment that would bring the stock on hand below the reserved quantity. | {must}
| [[FR-INV-007]]FR-INV-007 | The system shall announce every change of an available quantity, so that the catalog can refresh its copy. | {must}
| [[FR-INV-008]]FR-INV-008 | The system shall record every stock movement with its quantity, its cause and, for an adjustment, its author and reason. | {must}
|===

== Cart and favorites

.Strategy question
****
What strategy keeps a cart cheap and forgiving, while the order that comes out of it is strict?
****

A cart is a list of intentions.
It reserves nothing and promises nothing; its amounts are recomputed from the catalog every time it is read.
Strictness begins at checkout.

[cols="3,10,2", options="header"]
|===
| ID | Rule | Priority
| [[FR-CART-001]]FR-CART-001 | A customer shall have exactly one cart, kept between sessions and devices. | {must}
| [[FR-CART-002]]FR-CART-002 | A line shall be added or increased only for a published product, with a quantity between 1 and the maximum quantity per line; a cart shall hold no more than the maximum number of lines. | {must}
| [[FR-CART-003]]FR-CART-003 | Adding a product that is already in the cart shall increase the quantity of its line instead of creating a second line. | {must}
| [[FR-CART-004]]FR-CART-004 | The system shall refuse a line quantity above the available quantity known to the catalog when the change is made. | {must}
| [[FR-CART-005]]FR-CART-005 | A line total shall equal the current catalog price multiplied by the quantity; the cart total shall equal the sum of the totals of the lines that are not flagged. | {must}
| [[FR-CART-006]]FR-CART-006 | The cart counter shall equal the sum of the quantities of the lines that are not flagged: it counts units, not products. | {must}
| [[FR-CART-007]]FR-CART-007 | When an order is confirmed, the system shall remove from the cart the quantities that were ordered; when an order is rejected, the cart shall be left as it is. | {must}
| [[FR-CART-008]]FR-CART-008 | A product shall appear at most once in a customer's favorites, which shall hold no more than the maximum number of favorites. | {must}
| [[FR-CART-009]]FR-CART-009 | A favorite whose product is out of stock or retired shall stay in the list, flagged as unavailable. | {must}
| [[FR-CART-010]]FR-CART-010 | A cart line whose product is no longer published, or whose quantity exceeds the available quantity known to the catalog, shall be flagged as unavailable; the checkout shall be refused while the cart holds a flagged line. | {must}
|===

== Order

.Strategy question
****
What strategy ensures an order is either fully honored or leaves no trace on the customer's money and the store's stock?
****

[cols="3,10,2", options="header"]
|===
| ID | Rule | Priority
| [[FR-ORD-001]]FR-ORD-001 | An order shall be created from the customer's own cart, with at least one line, no flagged line, a shipping address and a payment token. | {must}
| [[FR-ORD-002]]FR-ORD-002 | The unit prices of an order shall be copied from the catalog when the order is placed, and shall be the prices of that order forever. | {must}
| [[FR-ORD-003]]FR-ORD-003 | If the price or the status of a line differs from what the customer reviewed, or if the available quantity known to the catalog is lower than the quantity of a line, the system shall create no order and shall return the differences. | {must}
| [[FR-ORD-004]]FR-ORD-004 | The order total shall equal the sum, over the lines, of the unit price multiplied by the quantity. Shipping is free and prices include VAT. | {must}
| [[FR-ORD-005]]FR-ORD-005 | The system shall assign each order a unique order number that the customer can read and quote. | {must}
| [[FR-ORD-006]]FR-ORD-006 | The system shall reserve the stock of an order before requesting its payment, and shall never request the payment of an order whose stock was not reserved. | {must}
| [[FR-ORD-007]]FR-ORD-007 | An order shall become `CONFIRMED` only when its payment is captured and its reservation is confirmed. | {must}
| [[FR-ORD-008]]FR-ORD-008 | An order that cannot be confirmed shall become `REJECTED`, with exactly one reason, and only once it holds no payment: none was captured, the payment was canceled at the provider, or it was refunded. Its reservation is released; a reservation whose release did not get through expires by itself. | {must}
| [[FR-ORD-009]]FR-ORD-009 | Submitting the same checkout more than once shall return the same order and create nothing new. | {must}
| [[FR-ORD-010]]FR-ORD-010 | The status of an order shall change only along the transitions below; a final status shall never change, whatever message or request arrives afterwards. | {must}
| [[FR-ORD-011]]FR-ORD-011 | The lines, prices and shipping address of an order shall not change once the order is placed. | {must}
| [[FR-ORD-012]]FR-ORD-012 | A customer shall be able to cancel an order only while it is `CONFIRMED`. The order shall become `CANCELING`, its stock shall be returned and its payment refunded in full, and only then shall it become `CANCELED`. | {should}
| [[FR-ORD-013]]FR-ORD-013 | Only an administrator shall move an order from `CONFIRMED` to `SHIPPED` and from `SHIPPED` to `DELIVERED`. | {should}
| [[FR-ORD-014]]FR-ORD-014 | When a refund keeps failing, the system shall set the order to `UNDER_REVIEW` and alert an administrator; it shall never record as refunded a payment that was not. | {must}
| [[FR-ORD-015]]FR-ORD-015 | When the order deadline expires on a `PENDING` order whose payment is not known to be captured, the system shall cancel the payment at the provider and reject the order with the reason `TIMED_OUT`; if the payment proves to be captured, the order shall be carried through instead. | {must}
| [[FR-ORD-016]]FR-ORD-016 | An order that is still `PENDING` when the review delay expires shall become `UNDER_REVIEW`: its automatic processing stops and an administrator is alerted. | {must}
| [[FR-ORD-017]]FR-ORD-017 | A customer shall have at most one `PENDING` order: while it is pending, another checkout shall be refused and shall point to that order. | {must}
| [[FR-ORD-018]]FR-ORD-018 | An administrator's decision on an order under review shall obey the rules of the automatic flow: the order is confirmed only if its payment is captured and its stock secured; it is rejected, with the reason `ADMIN_DECISION`, only once it holds no payment. | {must}
|===

== Payment

.Strategy question
****
What strategy makes it impossible to charge a customer twice, or to keep money for an order that does not exist?
****

[cols="3,10,2", options="header"]
|===
| ID | Rule | Priority
| [[FR-PAY-001]]FR-PAY-001 | An order shall be charged at most once, whatever the number of retries, duplicate requests or restarts. | {must}
| [[FR-PAY-002]]FR-PAY-002 | The amount charged shall equal the order total, in EUR. | {must}
| [[FR-PAY-003]]FR-PAY-003 | The platform shall never receive, store or log card data; a payment is requested with a token issued by the payment provider. | {must}
| [[FR-PAY-004]]FR-PAY-004 | When the provider declines a payment, the system shall record it as `REFUSED` with the provider's reason and shall give the customer a general explanation only. | {must}
| [[FR-PAY-005]]FR-PAY-005 | When the provider does not answer, the system shall record the payment as `UNKNOWN`. Before its order is rejected, the payment shall be canceled at the provider, so that it can no longer be captured; a payment that proves to be captured is never canceled: its order is carried through, or the payment is refunded. | {must}
| [[FR-PAY-006]]FR-PAY-006 | A refund shall return the full captured amount, and a payment shall be refunded at most once. | {must}
| [[FR-PAY-007]]FR-PAY-007 | The simulated provider shall answer according to the payment token, so that every outcome can be reproduced on demand. | {must}
|===

In a real store the customer types card details into a form served by the payment provider, which returns a token to the storefront; the platform only ever sees the token.
In this release the storefront offers the tokens below as test means of payment.

.Tokens understood by the simulated payment provider ({FR-PAY-007})
[%unbreakable, cols="4,11", options="header"]
|===
| Token | Behavior
| `tok_ok` | Captures at once.
| `tok_declined` | Refuses: insufficient funds.
| `tok_slow` | Captures after a delay shorter than the order deadline.
| `tok_timeout` | Never answers the charge and does not capture. A cancellation succeeds.
| `tok_late_capture` | Never answers the charge, but captures. A cancellation answers "already captured".
| `tok_blackout` | Answers nothing at all: neither the charge, nor a status query, nor a cancellation.
| `tok_error` | Fails with a technical error on every attempt, and does not capture.
| `tok_flaky` | Fails twice, then captures.
| `tok_refund_fails` | Captures at once; every refund fails.
|===

== Notification

.Strategy question
****
What strategy informs the customer reliably without ever putting an e-mail in the way of an order?
****

[cols="3,10,2", options="header"]
|===
| ID | Rule | Priority

| [[FR-NOT-002]]FR-NOT-002 | The system shall send at most one e-mail per event, even when the event is received several times. | {must}
| [[FR-NOT-003]]FR-NOT-003 | A failure to send an e-mail shall never change, delay or reject an order; the sending shall be retried, then recorded as failed. | {must}
