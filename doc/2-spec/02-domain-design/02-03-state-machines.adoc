== Why two machines for one order

.Strategy question
****
What strategy shows the customer four simple statuses while the system juggles a dozen technical steps?
****

An order has two lives.
Its *business status* is what the customer, the administrator and every other document talk about: pending, confirmed, rejected, under review, and so on.
Its *saga state* is where the processing stands: which request was sent, which answer is awaited, which deadline is running.

Mixing them would force a choice between a status list nobody outside the team understands, and a processing model too coarse to recover from a crash.
They are therefore two machines: the saga decides, and a transition of the saga may — or may not — move the order.
While a saga is working, the order simply stays `PENDING` or `CANCELING`.

No state machine engine is used.
Each machine is a plain domain class holding its state and a method per event; an unexpected event in a given state is refused or ignored by that method, and every pair of state and event is covered by a unit test.

== Order status

.Strategy question
****
What strategy guarantees that an order can never jump to a status the business did not allow?
****

plantuml::../01-business-needs/diagrams/order-status.puml[format=svg, align=center]

.Transitions of the order status ({FR-ORD-010})
[cols="3,3,7,5", options="header"]
|===
| From | To | When | Guard
| – | `PENDING` | A customer's checkout is accepted | Cart unchanged since the review; no flagged line; no other pending order of that customer
| `PENDING` | `CONFIRMED` | The place-order saga completes | Payment captured and reservation confirmed
| `PENDING` | `REJECTED` | The place-order saga is compensated | The order holds no payment; exactly one reason is given
| `PENDING` | `UNDER_REVIEW` | The review delay expires, or a refund keeps failing | –
| `UNDER_REVIEW` | `CONFIRMED` | A retried step succeeds, or an administrator confirms | Payment captured and reservation confirmed
| `UNDER_REVIEW` | `REJECTED` | A retried step ends in a rejection, or an administrator rejects | The order holds no payment
| `CONFIRMED` | `SHIPPED` | An administrator marks it shipped | –
| `SHIPPED` | `DELIVERED` | An administrator marks it delivered | –
| `CONFIRMED` | `CANCELING` | The customer cancels | –
| `CANCELING` | `CANCELED` | The cancel-order saga completes | Stock returned and payment refunded
| `CANCELING` | `UNDER_REVIEW` | The refund keeps failing, or the review delay expires | –
| `UNDER_REVIEW` | `CANCELED` | A retried refund succeeds | The order was being canceled; payment refunded
|===

Any other pair is refused.
In particular nothing leaves `REJECTED`, `DELIVERED` or `CANCELED`, whatever message or request arrives.

== Place-order saga

.Strategy question
****
What strategy lets an order placement stop at any instant and resume exactly where it was?
****

The saga state is saved, in the same transaction as the request it sends, each time it changes.
After a crash the service reads the state and knows what was last asked and what is awaited; nothing has to be remembered in memory.

plantuml::diagrams/saga-place-order.puml[format=svg, align=center]

plantuml::diagrams/saga-place-order-review.puml[format=svg, align=center]

.States of the place-order saga
[%unbreakable, cols="4,8,3", options="header"]
|===
| State | Meaning | Order status
| `RESERVING_STOCK` | The reservation was requested; the answer is awaited | `PENDING`
| `CHARGING_PAYMENT` | The stock is held; the charge was requested | `PENDING`
| `CANCELING_PAYMENT` | No payment answer at the order deadline; the cancellation was requested | `PENDING`
| `CONFIRMING_STOCK` | The payment is captured; the confirmation of the reservation was requested | `PENDING`
| `REFUNDING_PAYMENT` | The order cannot be honored although paid; the refund was requested | `UNDER_REVIEW`
| `SUSPENDED` | Automatic processing has stopped; an administrator decides | `UNDER_REVIEW`
| `COMPLETED` | Final. The order is paid and its stock secured | `CONFIRMED`
| `COMPENSATED` | Final. Everything done was undone, or there was nothing to undo | `REJECTED`
|===

.Transitions of the place-order saga
[cols="4,5,6,4", options="header"]
|===
| In state | On | The saga does | Next state

| (start)
| The order is created
| Sets the order deadline and the review deadline. Sends `ReserveStock`.
| `RESERVING_STOCK`

.3+| `RESERVING_STOCK`
| `StockReserved`
| Sends `ChargePayment`.
| `CHARGING_PAYMENT`

| `StockReservationRejected`
| Rejects the order: `OUT_OF_STOCK`.
| `COMPENSATED`

| Order deadline
| Sends `ReleaseReservation`. Rejects the order: `TIMED_OUT`.
| `COMPENSATED`

.3+| `CHARGING_PAYMENT`
| `PaymentCaptured`
| Sends `ConfirmReservation`.
| `CONFIRMING_STOCK`

| `PaymentRefused`
| Sends `ReleaseReservation`. Rejects the order: `PAYMENT_REFUSED`.
| `COMPENSATED`

| `PaymentOutcomeUnknown`, or the order deadline
| Sends `CancelPayment`. When the payment service itself reports that nothing is established, the saga does not wait for the deadline.
| `CANCELING_PAYMENT`

.2+| `CANCELING_PAYMENT`
| `PaymentCanceled`
| Sends `ReleaseReservation`. Rejects the order: `TIMED_OUT`.
| `COMPENSATED`

| `PaymentCaptured`
| Sends `ConfirmReservation`.
| `CONFIRMING_STOCK`

.2+| `CONFIRMING_STOCK`
| `ReservationConfirmed`
| Confirms the order. Publishes `OrderConfirmed`.
| `COMPLETED`

| `ReservationConfirmationFailed`
| Sends `RefundPayment`.
| `REFUNDING_PAYMENT`

.2+| `REFUNDING_PAYMENT`
| `PaymentRefunded`
| Rejects the order: `OUT_OF_STOCK`, or `ADMIN_DECISION` when an administrator asked for it.
| `COMPENSATED`

| `PaymentRefundFailed`
| Puts the order under review, or leaves it there. Raises an alert.
| `SUSPENDED`

| `CHARGING_PAYMENT`, `CANCELING_PAYMENT`, `CONFIRMING_STOCK`
| Review deadline
| Remembers the step in progress. Puts the order under review. Raises an alert.
| `SUSPENDED`

.3+| `SUSPENDED`
| The administrator retries
| Sends again the request of the remembered step.
| The remembered state

| The administrator confirms
| Refused unless the payment is captured. Sends `ConfirmReservation`.
| `CONFIRMING_STOCK`

| The administrator rejects
| Payment captured: sends `RefundPayment`. Payment unknown: sends `CancelPayment`. Sends `ReleaseReservation`.
| `REFUNDING_PAYMENT` or `CANCELING_PAYMENT`
|===

.Events that arrive when they are no longer expected
[%unbreakable, cols="5,10", options="header"]
|===
| Situation | Rule
| A duplicate of an event already handled | Ignored: the message was already recorded, or the saga is no longer in the state that expects it.
| `StockReserved` after the saga was compensated at the order deadline | The saga sends `ReleaseReservation` once more. Releasing is harmless when repeated.
| `ReservationReleased`, `ReservationExpired` | Recorded for information. The saga never waits for them.
| `PaymentCaptured` after the saga was compensated | Cannot happen: a payment is canceled before its order is rejected. Should it happen all the same, the saga sends `RefundPayment` and raises an alert.
| Any event in `COMPLETED` or `COMPENSATED` | Ignored.
|===

== Cancel-order saga

.Strategy question
****
What strategy undoes a confirmed order with the same guarantees as placing it?
****

plantuml::diagrams/saga-cancel-order.puml[format=svg, align=center]

.Transitions of the cancel-order saga
[%unbreakable, cols="4,5,6,4", options="header"]
|===
| In state | On | The saga does | Next state
| (start) | The cancellation is accepted | Sets the order to `CANCELING` and the review deadline. Sends `ReturnStock`. | `RETURNING_STOCK`
| `RETURNING_STOCK` | `StockReturned` | Sends `RefundPayment`. | `REFUNDING_PAYMENT`
| `REFUNDING_PAYMENT` | `PaymentRefunded` | Sets the order to `CANCELED`. Publishes `OrderCanceled`. | `COMPLETED`
| `REFUNDING_PAYMENT` | `PaymentRefundFailed` | Puts the order under review. Raises an alert. | `SUSPENDED`
| Any working state | Review deadline | Remembers the step. Puts the order under review. | `SUSPENDED`
| `SUSPENDED` | The administrator retries | Sends again the request of the remembered step. | The remembered state
|===

The stock is returned before the refund is asked, so that a refund in difficulty never keeps units out of sale.

== Payment

.Strategy question
****
What strategy makes sure a payment is never in two states at once, such as refunded and capturable?
****

plantuml::diagrams/payment-status.puml[format=svg, align=center]

.Transitions of the payment status
[%unbreakable, cols="3,3,9", options="header"]
|===
| From | To | When
| – | `PENDING` | A charge is requested for an order that has no payment yet
| `PENDING` | `CAPTURED` | The provider accepts
| `PENDING` | `REFUSED` | The provider declines
| `PENDING` | `UNKNOWN` | The provider does not answer after the retries, or keeps failing
| `UNKNOWN` | `CANCELED` | A cancellation is requested and the provider accepts it
| `UNKNOWN` | `CAPTURED` | The provider reports the capture, or answers "already captured" to a cancellation
| `CAPTURED` | `REFUNDED` | A refund is requested and the provider accepts it
|===

A refund that fails leaves the payment `CAPTURED`: the status always says where the money is.
A request that does not fit the current status is not an error but an answer: asking to charge a captured payment answers "captured", asking to refund a refunded payment answers "refunded" ({FR-PAY-001}, {FR-PAY-006}).

== Reservation and product

.Strategy question
****
What strategy keeps the small lifecycles as explicit as the large ones?
****

plantuml::../01-business-needs/diagrams/reservation-lifecycle.puml[format=svg, align=center]

A reservation that is confirmed, released or expired stays so.
